title: New penguin path opens at Zoo Zürich

The penguin parade returns this winter. When the temperature drops below 10 °C, the king penguins
take a walk through the zoo grounds around 13:30. The new path makes it easier for visitors to watch.
The parade is weather dependent and may be cancelled at short notice. Visit the opening-hours page for
the current opening times of the zoo.
