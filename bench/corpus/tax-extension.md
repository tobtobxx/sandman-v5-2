title: Tax return deadline and extensions – Canton of Zurich
keywords: Steuererklärung Fristerstreckung Frist Verlängerung Steueramt Kanton Zürich tax return deadline extension

The deadline for the tax return is 31 March. Private persons can request an extension online
free of charge until 30 September. A further extension until 30 November is possible for a fee of CHF 20
and must be requested before 30 September. Requests are made in the online portal with your
registration number. If you use an accountant, the accountant can request the extension for you.
