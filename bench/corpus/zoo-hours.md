Plan your visit to Zoo Zürich. The zoo is open every day of the year, including public holidays.
Our Masoala Rainforest hall and the Kaeng Krachan Elephant Park are included in every ticket.
Please note that some indoor houses close 30 minutes before the zoo itself.

Summer season (March to October): the zoo is open daily from 9:00 to 18:00.
Winter season (November to February): the zoo is open daily from 9:00 to 17:00.
Last admission is one hour before closing.

Tickets: adults CHF 29, children (6–15) CHF 15, children under 6 free. Annual passes are available at the main entrance.
Getting here: take tram 6 to the terminus "Zoo". Parking is limited; we recommend public transport.
