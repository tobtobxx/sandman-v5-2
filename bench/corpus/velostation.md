title: Velostation Nord – your bike shop at the main station

Velostation Nord has been part of the station district since 1998. What started as a small bicycle
parking garage with a single workbench has grown into one of the busiest bike workshops in town.
Every morning, several hundred commuters leave their bikes in our guarded parking hall, and many of
them drop off a bike for a quick check before catching their train. Our team of eight mechanics
includes two master mechanics and three apprentices, and we train new mechanics every year together
with the regional vocational school.

Our history. In the early years we mostly repaired city bikes and the occasional racing bike. The
workshop was in the basement of the old post building, and customers had to carry their bikes down a
narrow staircase. In 2006 we moved to the ground floor of the new station building, which gave us
room for six repair stands, a small shop and a parking hall for 800 bikes. Since then we have
expanded twice. In 2014 we opened a second parking hall on the east side of the station, and in 2021
we rebuilt the workshop to make room for cargo bikes, which have become very popular with families
in the district.

Parking. The parking hall is open around the clock for subscribers. A monthly subscription costs
CHF 20, a yearly subscription CHF 180. Day tickets are available at the machine next to the entrance.
Subscribers can use the air pump and the self-service tool wall for free. Lockers for helmets and
bags can be rented by the month.

Rental bikes. We rent city bikes, cargo bikes and tandems by the hour or by the day. Rental bikes
must be reserved at least one day in advance in summer. A valid ID is required. Children's seats and
trailers can be added for a small fee.

Sustainability. Our workshop runs on electricity from the station's solar roof. We recycle old tubes,
tyres and chains, and we sell refurbished second-hand bikes that come with a six-month guarantee.
Once a year we hold a bike market where you can sell your old bike or find a new one.

Opening hours. The shop and workshop are open Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 19:00 and Saturday from
9:00 to 16:00. The parking hall is open around the clock.

Workshop services. We repair all common types of bicycles: city bikes, road bikes, mountain bikes,
children's bikes and cargo bikes. Small repairs such as a flat tyre are usually done while you wait.
For larger repairs, please drop the bike off in the morning and pick it up in the evening.
A basic service costs CHF 89 and includes brakes, gears, tyre pressure and a safety check.

E-bikes. Yes, we repair e-bikes. Our mechanics are certified for Bosch, Shimano Steps and Brose
drive systems. An e-bike service costs CHF 149 and includes a software update of the drive unit and
a battery check. We do not repair batteries ourselves; we send them to the manufacturer.
