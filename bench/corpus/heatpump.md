title: How loud is a heat pump?

Modern air-to-water heat pumps produce between 35 and 55 dB(A) at a distance of one metre.
Most cantons require that a heat pump stays below 45 dB(A) at night at the neighbour's property line.
Placement matters: a unit in a corner reflects sound and can be up to 6 dB louder.
