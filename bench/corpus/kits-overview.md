title: The best drip irrigation kits for raised beds (2026)

Drip irrigation saves water and time. We looked at the kits most often sold in Switzerland for
raised beds and small vegetable gardens.

1. Gardena Micro-Drip starter set – the classic. Easy to extend, works with a tap timer.
2. Hozelock Easy Drip kit – cheaper and simple to install, but fewer extension parts.
3. AquaLine Basic kit – a budget kit from a DIY store brand with 20 drippers.
4. Claber Oasis – a gravity kit with a water tank, for gardens without a tap.

For raised beds with a tap nearby, the first three are the most common choices. Prices and coverage
are listed on each manufacturer's page.
