title: Bike shops in the city – an overview

Looking for a bike shop? Here are the most popular ones. Velostation Nord at the main station offers
guarded parking, rentals and a large workshop. Radwerk Süd specialises in road bikes and bike fitting.
Pedal & Co in the old town sells refurbished city bikes. Check each shop's website for services and prices.
